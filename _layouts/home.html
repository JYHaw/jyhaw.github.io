Highlights

  • Technical lead for Singapore’s National Quantum-Safe Network
  • Editor of Singapore’s reference specification on QKD networks
  • Contributor to ITU-T and ETSI quantum technology standardisation
  • Researcher in quantum-safe technologies and quantum photonics engineering

Research interests

Quantum communication technologies

QKD networks, quantum random number generators, hybrid quantum-safe architectures, and post-quantum cryptography deployment.

Quantum photonics engineering

Free-space, fibre-based, and integrated quantum photonic platforms for discrete and continuous-variable quantum state generation and detection.

Selected work

  • Deployment of QKD links in Singapore, including a data-centre QKD link and QKD-secured VPN demonstrations.
  • Application-layer integration of more than 15 quantum-safe use cases and proofs of concept.
  • Development of local and international standards for secure QKD network deployment and protocol frameworks.
